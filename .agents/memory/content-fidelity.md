name: Portfolio content fidelity
description: Keep separate projects and their outcomes distinct within each employer's portfolio story.
---

Do not combine separate workstreams into one causal story. Pair each result with its specific source activity, population, and scope; sharing an employer does not establish a causal relationship.

**Why:** The user corrected a portfolio rewrite that grouped separate classroom, schoolwide, research, and process outcomes too closely.

**How to apply:** When rewriting résumé content into challenge/approach/result sections, preserve the boundaries between source initiatives and label separate workstreams explicitly. Keep estimates qualified and do not invent relationships between metrics.

Pair each initiative's action directly with its own outcome when that avoids repeating labels. Introduce the actual responsibilities or situation, not a count of workstreams.

**Why:** The user confirmed the separated initiatives were factually accurate and requested a more natural, concise presentation without changing those distinctions.

**How to apply:** Use short initiative headings with corresponding actions and results together; do not force an employer's distinct projects into one challenge/action/result narrative.
