# Change 1: Improve the Work Experience page

## Goal

Make my Work Experience page function more like a personal portfolio and less like a copy of my résumé. I want visitors to quickly understand the problems I worked on, what I did, and the results.

## Planned changes

- Feature my three most relevant experiences: NetApp, NextBound, and KIPP NYC.
- Give each featured experience clearer visual hierarchy.
- Organize the content around the challenge/problem, my work or approach, and the outcome rather than simply displaying résumé bullets.
- Keep my other professional experience in a more concise “Earlier Experience” section.
- Preserve the existing site's responsive, accessible design.
- Do not invent or alter any employers, roles, dates, achievements, or metrics. Only use information already provided in my résumé/site.

## Why

My current Work Experience page contains strong information, but it reads similarly to a traditional résumé. This change will make the page easier to scan and better demonstrate how I approach problems and create results.
