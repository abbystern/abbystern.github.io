# A people person who loves a good system.

I’m Abby Stern, an MBA student at UC Berkeley Haas interested in how technology, data, and good operating systems can help people do their best work.

Before business school, I spent four years teaching fifth-grade English in the Bronx. Teaching taught me a lot about people, but it also made me interested in systems. I found myself constantly asking why something wasn’t working, digging into the data, and building a better process—whether that meant redesigning how we taught reading or creating new ways to track student progress. That work ultimately led me to business school and to strategy and operations.

At Haas, I’ve been exploring that same intersection of people, technology, and operations in very different settings. I worked with NextBound, an AI-enabled coaching startup, and spent my summer at NetApp working on a large-scale transformation of its professional services organization. At NetApp, I mapped and redesigned processes across teams and geographies, looking for places where technology and automation could remove unnecessary work while keeping the parts that actually benefit from human judgment.

The throughline in my career is pretty simple: I like messy problems. I like talking to the people closest to them, figuring out what’s actually going wrong, and turning that ambiguity into something clearer and more useful. I’m especially interested in strategy and operations, AI, and the challenge of translating new technology into systems that work in the real world.

Outside of work and school, I’m a lifelong reader and a certified yoga teacher. You can usually find me with a book, running around the Bay Area, or planning my next trip.
