# A people person who loves a good system.

Before business school, I spent four years teaching fifth-grade English in the Bronx. Teaching taught me a lot about people, but it also made me interested in systems. I found myself constantly asking why something wasn’t working, digging into the data, and building a better process, whether that meant redesigning how we taught reading or creating new ways to track student progress. That work ultimately led me to business school and to strategy and operations.

At Haas, I worked with NextBound, an AI-enabled coaching startup, and spent my summer at NetApp working on a large-scale transformation of its professional services organization.

Outside of work and school, I’m a 200-hour certified yoga teacher and a lifelong bookworm always searching for the next great read. You can usually find me running around the Bay Area or planning my next trip.
